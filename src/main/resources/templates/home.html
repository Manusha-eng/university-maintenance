Welcome to the Maintenance Portal

This system allows students, lecturers and non-academic staff to report maintenance issues within the Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna.

Maintenance complaints may include electrical faults, plumbing issues, damaged furniture, classroom equipment failures, laboratory facilities and other university maintenance requirements.
